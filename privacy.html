Privacy policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Omly Meditation (“Omly”, “we”, “us”) handles information in the iOS app and on this website. For privacy questions, contact jayantvishwakarmajv2002@gmail.com.

The short version

The current app stores your profile, meditation history, and imported music on your device. It does not require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or send that information to a developer-operated server.

Information stored in the app

  • Profile details: the name, email address, and meditation intention you choose to enter. They personalize the app; the email field is not used for account registration or marketing.
  • Meditation history: saved session dates, start times, and durations, used to display your session history.
  • Audio and preferences: copies of music files you import, their display names, and your selected track, used for meditation playback.

These items are stored in the app’s local storage. You can use the app without filling in your profile.

Files and permissions

When you import music, you choose a file through Apple’s document picker. Omly reads the selected file and copies it into its own storage; it does not scan your other files. If you choose a cloud provider, that provider handles the file transfer under its own privacy terms. Omly does not request microphone, photo library, contacts, location, or Apple Music library access for this feature.

Collection, tracking, and sharing

The current app does not transmit your profile, imported audio, or session history to us. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell this information or use it for cross-app tracking.

Apple may process App Store, device backup, and diagnostic information according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. We do not control those services.

Storage, retention, and deletion

App data remains on your device until you change it or remove it. You can edit or clear your profile fields in Settings. Deleting the app using iOS Delete App removes its local data, including imported audio and session history; offloading the app preserves that data.

Depending on your Apple settings, app data may be included in device or iCloud backups. Manage or delete those backups through Apple’s settings. We do not operate a server-side copy of your app data and cannot recover local data after deletion.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and investigate issues. Email is processed by our email provider. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle your request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence by emailing the address below.

This website

These pages use no analytics, advertising scripts, embedded trackers, or cookies set by us. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical request information for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own policies.

Children’s privacy

Omly does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. Please do not include a child’s personal details in support messages. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Your choices and requests

You control the details you enter and the files you import. For questions or requests about support correspondence, including access, correction, or deletion, email jayantvishwakarmajv2002@gmail.com. Because app data is stored locally, you manage it directly on your device.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when the app’s data practices change. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
