Getting started

How do I add and select my own music?

Open Settings, choose “Add music file…”, and select a file from Files, iCloud Drive, or another provider available in the Files picker. After import, the track is selected automatically. You can tap another track to choose it for your next meditation.

Why won’t my audio file import?

The file must contain audio that your device can play. Try an unprotected MP3, M4A, WAV, or AIFF file. Some codecs, damaged files, and protected music cannot be played. Download cloud files first if necessary, or convert the audio to a compatible format.

When does my selected music play?

Your selected track starts when you start meditation and repeats during the session. Check the device volume and the session’s sound control if you do not hear it.

Where can I see my meditation history?

Open the summary screen and select a date. Use the month selector to browse other months. Saved sessions for that day appear with their time and duration. Sessions shorter than your selected minimum duration may not be saved.

What happens if I lock my phone or leave the app?

The current version resets the meditation session when the app becomes inactive or moves to the background. Keep Omly in the foreground while meditating.

How do I change my name or intention?

Open Settings, edit your details, and tap Done. Your saved name appears in your greeting. These details are stored locally; entering an email does not create an account or subscribe you to messages.

How do I remove my information?

You can clear your profile fields in Settings and tap Done. To remove all local app data, including imported music and session history, delete the app using iOS Delete App. Offloading the app keeps its data. Device backups are managed separately through Apple’s settings.
